Questions

Asked by accountants, answered plainly.

Is Zycount real double-entry accounting?

Yes. Every transaction is a journal with at least two lines that must balance before it posts. Invoices, bills, receipts, payroll and depreciation all generate their journals automatically, and you can see each one before and after it posts.

Can I fix a mistake in a posted entry?

You reverse it. Zycount posts an equal-and-opposite entry linked to the original, then you post the correct one. The ledger keeps the full story, which is exactly what an auditor wants to see.

How does it handle SST and payroll deductions?

Tax codes carry the rate and post output and input tax to separate accounts. Payroll computes EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB from versioned rate tables. Both are configuration, so you can update rates when they change — and you should verify them against the authorities before filing.

Can I move my existing books in?

Yes. Import your chart of accounts, customers, suppliers, products and opening balances from Excel or CSV, with validation and a preview before anything is written.

Can I try it right now?

The demo workspace is open: sign in with admin@zycount.test and Zycount!Demo2026, or create your own account. Try the accountant and then the auditor login — what you can do changes with the role.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export any report or list to CSV or PDF at any time. Zycount is built on open-source components (PostgreSQL, Next.js, NestJS) and can be self-hosted.
