How it works

From a transaction to a signed statement

Four steps, the same every day — whether you post one entry or a thousand.

1

Record

Raise an invoice or bill, or key a manual journal — with validation so no blank line or unbalanced entry slips through.

2

Post

The double entry hits the ledger automatically, tax codes and SST applied, and the trial balance updates on the spot.

3

Reconcile

Match bank lines against the ledger, clear open items, and let Zycount flag anything that doesn't tie out.

4

Report

Open the P&L, balance sheet and cash flow — already agreeing with the ledger — and export or hand them off.
